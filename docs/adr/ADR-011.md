### ADR-011 — Grounding is enforced in code, not requested in the prompt (amended by proposals 003 and 006)
- Retrieved chunks are passed with short IDs (`[C3]`). Output schema requires `citations: list[str]` per claim/card.
- `citations.py` rejects any ID not in the provided context, any card with zero citations, and any answer whose claims are uncited. One retry with the error message; then drop (cards) or abstain (answers). Dropped counts are logged and reported.
- **Abstention** (amended by proposals 003 and 006): two stages. Stage 1, no LLM call: refuse only if the top **dense cosine similarity** < τ_cos **and** the normalized top BM25 score (top BM25 score divided by the number of query tokens) < τ_bm25. RRF scores are rank-only and the reranker is off (ADR-006 result), so neither can be thresholded. (τ_cos, τ_bm25) are calibrated on dev in M6: the pair that refuses the most unanswerable queries subject to a false-refusal rate on answerable queries of at most 5%; ties go to the lowest thresholds. Stage 2: the answer schema has a model-side `insufficient` flag; if true the answer must have no claims and the response is a refusal. Reported rows: cosine only, cosine + BM25, and the full two-stage (what ships); the primary metric is the false-refusal rate.
- At most 6 chunks in context, highest-ranked first. *Evidence:* "Lost in the Middle" (Liu et al., TACL 2024) — models use information at the start/end of context far better than the middle; fewer, better chunks beat many marginal ones.
*Amended by proposal 003, `docs/proposals/003-m4-findings.md` (approved by the owner): abstention uses the top dense cosine similarity instead of a reranker score. Amended again by proposal 006, `docs/proposals/006-two-stage-abstention.md` (approved by the owner): two-stage abstention.*
*Result (M6, dev split, `results/abstention_calibration_dev.json`): the pre-registered rule selected thresholds that refuse nothing. With at most 5% false refusals (at most 2 of 54 answerable dev queries) no threshold pair catches any of the 6 unanswerable dev queries; catching 1 needs at least 5 false refusals, catching all 6 needs at least 13. Stage 1 is therefore inactive on this corpus, and abstention rests on the model-side flag (stage 2).*
